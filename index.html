香港科技大学数学系 · 博士后研究员

我的研究主要围绕波散射与反问题，关注复杂介质中波传播的高精度数值计算、快速算法及其理论分析，并研究选择性聚焦、统计成像与分辨率问题。

My research focuses on wave scattering and inverse problems, including high-accuracy numerical methods, fast algorithms, and their theoretical analysis for wave propagation in complex media. I also study selective focusing, statistical imaging, and resolution analysis.
